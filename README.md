# dogshit-business-review

Vets business ideas before you sink time into them. Two skills — `business-spec`, `grill-business-spec` — turn a pitch into a written Idea Brief, then interrogate it until the wishful thinking's gone.

## Why

Ideas sound plausible as long as they stay a conversation. Writing forces specifics. Grilling forces honesty about known vs. assumed. Repo packages both steps as reusable skills.

## How

**1. `business-spec`** — pitch → Idea Brief: Problem, Solution, Target Customer, Market & Competition (real research, not guesses), Business Model (table — self-serve revenue separate from partnership revenue), Distribution, Why Us, Risks & Open Questions. Missing input stays blank, never invented.

**2. `grill-business-spec`** — runs right after, same session. Fills blanks first, one question at a time. Then stress-tests: is the launch-funding revenue stream credible? Is distribution a real channel or generic "SEO + social"? Are named competitors actual competitors? Can the target customer be reached and pay? Doc updates only after shared understanding — interview, not autowrite.

## Output

One markdown file per idea: `business-ideas/[idea-name-slugified].md`, titled `[Idea Name] — Idea Brief`. Working doc, skimmable in 90 seconds — not a pitch deck. Contains:

- Problem → solution → who pays → acquisition → what kills it
- Named, researched competitors, not assumed gaps
- Business Model table flagging which stream funds launch
- Risks & Open Questions sharpened by the grill, not just first-draft blind spots
- Next Steps, generated only post-grill

`business-ideas/` accumulates into a running log of every idea run through the process — survivors and casualties both.
